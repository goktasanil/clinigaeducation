# CliniGA Education: guided atlas

Brief grounded in the existing live site and the user's request to apply ScrollCraft. Decisions below are authored implementation choices, not invented interview answers.

User request: “ScrollCraft: etkileşimli web tasarımı. yap www.clinigaeducation.com için”.

## Eight brief topics

1. Tone: academic, personal, calm, forward-looking. Keep the current navy/teal identity, logo, Montserrat/Inter typography and translated service copy.
2. Journey: recognize the goal, choose a country, understand the five-step process, examine the existing expertise and scope, continue into real country guides or the portal, request a consultation.
3. Energy: an inviting photographic opening, quiet decision links, one deliberate roadmap peak, practical reading and a complete final action.
4. Feeling curve: possibility through real campus photography; agency through country selection; clarity through five connected process steps; confidence through scope and expertise; readiness through real guide and consultation links.
5. Signature move: the opening's selected destination remains selected in the lower discovery area and opens the correct existing country-filtered journal.
6. Range: preserve the established premium academic brand. Genuine architectural photography and a tangible route note replace space-like ornament.
7. Structure: distinct useful scenes in natural flow, with one desktop sticky roadmap. No worldflight or scroll hijacking.
8. Assets: existing logo and three already-reviewed Wikimedia campus photographs. Original visible credits and licenses preserved. No image generation or Kie account required.

## Grammar and layer contract

Custom grammar: **guided atlas**. Existing full navigation remains available; each scene answers a planning question. One pinned process spread is the peak, logistics remain in flow, and the ending is the real consultation action.

The stock grammars were considered: filmic hides useful entry points; chaptered editorial excludes the photographic opening; live surface would turn guidance into a fake app; continuous world would require invented geography; typographic poster discards photographic evidence; gallery makes guidance secondary to objects; split stage invents an unnecessary comparison; rhythmic cutlist is too fast for academic decisions.

| Plane                | Movement                                          | Role                                      |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Quiet atlas contours | Small independent parallax                        | Spatial context, no geographic data claim |
| Real campus frame    | Slow upward parallax and subtle fine-pointer tilt | Destination evidence, credited photograph |
| Route note           | Faster foreground parallax                        | Selected country and real guide link      |
| Heading and actions  | Stable                                            | Always readable and actionable            |

Opening: a full headline and architectural photograph. Midpoint: the route note separates from the slower frame. Exit: the scene resolves into practical entry points and the roadmap.

The process gets the largest intentional scroll span. No empty dwell scenes or invented statistics. All five steps remain readable before JavaScript, with paused/reduced motion, and on compact screens.

## Implementation and release boundary

Use the byte-for-byte upstream ScrollCraft engine with a scoped style bridge. Its unmodified CSS is retained as reference, not globally applied over the existing application. All generated or downloaded artifacts stay in this Git-backed project.

AGENTS.md requires a focused branch and pull request. Production merging and publication require an explicit user instruction. Authentication, leads, payments, full blog content and existing SEO metadata are outside this visual change.
